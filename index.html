Trail Book Purchase Model
Price a trail book acquisition — breakeven, NPV & IRR under four payment structures. $ AUD.
1Book Assumptions
Incl. GST grosses up every $ figure entered (trail, custom $ payments, clawback $) by 10% across all calculations.
Trail month = the month the trail figure comes from; its day-count annualises the price (÷ days × 365). Settlement month = when vendor payments & cashflow start counting.
$ at risk auto-fills as 100% of upfronts <12 mo old + 50% of upfronts 12–24 mo old (all aggregators assumed same tiers for now) — or type it directly. Expected clawback = rate × $ at risk, spread over the first 24 months.
2Deal & Hurdle
Purchase price = trail ÷ days in trail month × 365 × multiple. Accept = NPV positive when discounted at the required return.
3Payment Structure
Purchase price = sum of the amounts. The multiple input is ignored; the implied multiple is shown in the summary. Amounts are entered excl. GST — the GST toggle grosses them up with everything else.
If modelled revenue between two payments falls short of the guarantee (pro-rated to the gap), every $ of shortfall is deducted from the next instalment (2nd, 3rd, …), floored at $0. Requires a structure with more than one payment. Entered excl. GST.

Method. All $ inputs are entered excl. GST; selecting Incl. GST grosses trail, payments and clawback $ up by 10% consistently. Purchase price = monthly trail (GST-adjusted) ÷ days in trail month × 365 × multiple. Trail decays monthly at run-off/12 from settlement, reduced by arrears %. $ at risk of clawback = 100% of upfronts on loans <12 months old + 50% of upfronts 12–24 months old. Expected clawback (rate × $ at risk) is deducted evenly across the first 24 months. NPV discounts monthly cashflows at the required return / 12. IRR is solved on monthly cashflows and annualised (effective); with instalment structures the cashflow changes sign more than once and IRR can be ambiguous, so the Accept/Reject verdict is based on NPV > 0 at the required return. Revenue guarantee: for each instalment after the first, if modelled net trail collected since the previous payment falls short of the guarantee (pro-rated to the window length), the shortfall is deducted dollar-for-dollar from that instalment, floored at $0. Breakeven is the month cumulative net cashflow turns positive and stays positive. Model horizon 10 years (120 months).
